# Website Studio review guide

This is the new owner-requested CMS interface candidate. The public website returns to the approved v4.3 visual baseline while retaining its connected services. This build is not yet owner-approved or deployed.

1. Open the connected preview launcher, then sign in to Website Studio.
2. Choose **Pages**, find a page by name, and choose **Edit page**. Or choose **Edit the homepage** from Home.
3. Choose **Editing language** (English or Arabic). Choose **Page title & introduction** or select a section in the list. You can also click a section in the website preview. Change words, formatted text or approved images in the editing panel.
4. Choose **Save draft**. Your changes stay private. The middle preview updates to the real saved website design. **Preview → Open preview** opens a larger protected view. Preview language is independent of CMS interface language and edited language; its label is visible.
5. Choose **Publish…**. Check the named content, language and destination, acknowledge that you reviewed the draft, and choose **Publish this page**. In local review, only this local website changes. English and Arabic publish separately.
6. Saved menus, services, product and site settings offer a nearby **Preview / Publish…** bar when their dedicated writer returns the saved target. Other publishing/scheduling/history controls remain in **More website tools → Publishing & schedules**.

If you leave an unsaved page, choose **Save & continue**, **Keep editing**, or **Leave without saving**. The CMS will not publish unsaved edits. If someone changes the draft during your publish review, close the confirmation, reopen the page and review the latest content.

Use **Photos & files**, **Menus & footer**, **Services**, **Articles**, and **Visitors & reports** for common tasks. **Find anything** searches every permitted CMS tool. Less frequent content tools are under **More website tools**; staff access, security and brand settings are under **Settings & team**. Advanced controls preserve server-enforced permissions.

To restore an earlier publication, the frozen database rules require the current language's saved draft to be reviewed first. **Review complete — ready to publish** does not itself publish that draft. Then use **Restore an earlier published version**. Saved revisions are retained; no database migration or implicit draft approval is introduced.

Please inspect whether you can find and edit a page without an explanation, see the correct saved design, and clearly understand what will publish. This technical review does not establish usability for every age group; owner usability and manual accessibility acceptance remain open.
