# Concrete privacy/legal review profile — draft, not an owner decision

Proposed public wording: “When you submit an inquiry, iDigitalRoot uses the information you provide to review your request and contact you about it. Submitting the form does not create an account or a paid order. Optional audience measurement is off until you allow it on the current page. Browser Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control keep it off. Measurement does not include your inquiry answers, name or email, and this implementation stores no persistent browser visitor identifier.”

The contact form can collect name, email, university, country, phone/contact preference, selected goals, existing website/domain/social links, preferred languages/features/timing and optional notes. Some are optional. People should not submit passwords, identity documents, health/financial credentials or sensitive third-party information. The application stores accepted answers/contact permission, a receipt, request/idempotency metadata and a sanitized conversion-outbox event in Core D1. Analytics uses the separate D1 database and minimized event classes. Cloudflare necessarily handles connection metadata; the daily opaque rate identity is derived server-side from the transient provider address and is not exposed as a browser identity.

Proposed operational decision for owner review (not applied): retain unconverted inquiry data for 90 days unless a documented service/legal obligation requires a different period; retain raw cookieless analytics for 30 days and coarse aggregates for 12 months; specify security/audit retention separately with legal review. Deletion/anonymization, backups, disputes/legal holds and data-subject requests need reviewed jobs and an actual restore/deletion rehearsal. Existing fixtures are test data, not production retention approval.

Before this text can be published, the owner must supply/approve the contracting/legal entity, jurisdiction, contact and privacy-request address, controller/processor responsibilities, target age/minor policy, final retention/rights/deletion terms, geographic/data-residency obligations, terms of service and third-party disclosures. Domain/email/service prices, limits, renewals and support guarantees remain separate commercial decisions.

workers/LEGAL_APPROVALS.json intentionally contains no owner decision or approved hashes. Editing a legal document in CMS does not approve it. A later explicit decision must identify the exact EN/AR ready-revision content hashes and decision reference; only then can the authorized publisher apply the recorded approval and publish. No checkbox in this preview substitutes for legal approval.
