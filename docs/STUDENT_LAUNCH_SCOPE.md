# First commercial Student Edition: scope and delivery gates

Owner decision: **Full Toolbox and Student Workspace required for the first student release.** These capabilities are mandatory launch scope, not optional post-launch items. They are currently presentation concepts; opening the public website or CMS does not make them operational.

## Managed-service catalogue

The approved student website describes these seven service families. The table separates product direction from commercial terms that have not been approved.

| Service family | Intended first-release delivery | Remaining decisions/evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Professionally built personal website | Profile, education, CV presentation, portfolio/projects, selected achievements, contact and public links; approved design, own website CMS, responsive EN/AR as contracted | Exact plan/page limits, content responsibility, delivery/support terms, reusable site runtime/CMS and acceptance |
| Personal domain | Independent address, ownership/management record and renewal visibility | Registrar, registration cost, who owns/pays, transfer and renewal rules; not automatically free/included |
| Professional email | Domain-based email where the purchased plan permits it; setup/support/renewal information | Provider, mailbox/storage limits and cost; not automatically free/included |
| Search/discovery readiness | Crawlable pages, metadata, sitemap and supported structured information | Test actual outputs; no ranking, job, scholarship or discovery guarantees; this is not an AI chatbot |
| Protection and monitoring | Defined managed security/maintenance, verified technical status and recovery procedures | Guaranteed checks vs informational checks, cadence, alerts, retention, backup/restore and service commitments |
| Connected public links | User-selected social/professional links and contact actions | Safe links and verified ownership where required; no assumed paid social analytics or scraping |
| Controlled growth | Requested additional pages, languages, supported features and integrations | Separate scope/cost/approval; no promise of arbitrary integrations or automatic code generation |

Basic website analytics is a supporting capability under the accepted privacy/configuration contract. It needs real collection, storage and reports; sample CMS figures are not live visitor analytics. The public Root Check scanner remains gated until its real scanner/provider/safety contract is implemented. A Root Health service must report verified checks and unknown/unavailable states truthfully.

Plan classification remains Included / Free Integration / Paid Add-on / Custom Service / Future. The owner's tool decision fixes launch scope, not prices, paid-provider approvals, limits or contractual service guarantees. No invented prices or provider commitments were added.

Career Kit appears in both the Toolbox and Workspace navigation, so there are 28 promised feature positions but 27 distinct named capabilities. The same underlying Career Kit must serve both entry points.

## Client account and service delivery

The commercial offering also needs secure account access, website portfolio, project intake/approvals, support, service/renewal visibility and secure access to each website CMS. No public self-signup is introduced into Phase 1. Account provisioning/authentication follows the later accepted client/Admin contract.

Public tool marketing must match the deployed capability and sold entitlement. All student majors receive the full Toolbox under the frozen product direction; suggested layouts can differ. Future employee/entrepreneur/business profiles may have different service catalogues and supported capabilities, while retaining common account structure.

## Decisions still required at their implementation/release gates

- Package prices, service limits, renewal/support rules and domain/email provider costs.
- Supported output/export formats and sharing/access policies for each tool.
- Privacy, retention and deletion rules for academic, career, contact and budget information.
- Notification channels and exact Root Health guarantees.
- Future edition route/domain conventions and the concrete edition gateway/session architecture.

These decisions are not reasons to ask the owner to repeat the transfer package. Propose bounded options when each becomes material. No paid resources, provider mutation or production release is authorized by this scope document.
