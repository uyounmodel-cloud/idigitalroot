## 5. Write the customized continuation prompt after consolidating the release

Derive this prompt from the actual finalized handoff. It must be specific enough that the new conversation understands what it is receiving, what must be read, where we stopped, and what to do next.

5. Require examination of all substantive handoff documentation, including requirements, decisions, corrections, failures, open items, and current state.
6. Require a coverage record for the original source artifacts, distinguishing fully inspected, partially inspected, indexed only, inaccessible, and not yet inspected material.
7. Require the original sources governing the next action to be read before that action is taken.

11. Require missing material and access limits to be disclosed rather than filled with assumptions.

15. Direct the new chat to resume work already authorized without asking me to repeat information contained in the release. Where an actual approval or missing decision is required, ask only for that specific item.

Write the continuation prompt in a clear professional form, ready for me to paste unchanged after uploading the files. Fill in all facts and filenames that are available. Do not leave generic placeholders where the release provides the answer.
