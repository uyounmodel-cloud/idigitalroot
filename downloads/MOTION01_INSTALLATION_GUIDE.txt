# MOTION 01 — opening the new design

This is the new motion-design review, superseding ROOT02 visually while keeping its four-audience company structure and connected CMS. It is a review candidate, not an accepted production release.

1. Close an older preview using its launcher window. Leave its folder/data intact.
2. Download IDIGITALROOT_MOTION01_REVIEW_2026-10-16_R1.zip from this repository's downloads folder and extract into a new folder.
3. Run OPEN_CONNECTED_PREVIEW.cmd using your existing Windows+Ubuntu/WSL environment. Keep the window open until you finish reviewing. The public website and connected CMS open through the launcher. Confirm the MOTION 01 label; old shortcuts still open old designs.
4. Explore the homepage, the four audience pages, Services, How We Work and Knowledge. Try both English and Arabic, and narrow your browser to phone width. Each audience has its own accent colour and a relevant, editable quote-wizard starting point.
5. Move over the hero sculpture on a mouse/trackpad, scroll to see section reveals and hover the audience panels. The pause/play control beside the language switch stops decorative movement and remembers the choice. Your device's Reduce Motion setting takes priority. Navigation and text remain usable without JavaScript; the protected CMS draft preview is deliberately static.
6. In the CMS choose a page, edit a heading or paragraph, Save & preview, then inspect the protected saved version. Saving does not alter the published website. Publish only through the named page/language review confirmation. Menu/footer/article/service controls retain their own scopes. The CMS console design remains the same.

## Your review changes

MOTION01 can copy compatible ROOT02 review data from the same launcher's native state storage into its own separate candidate folder. It does not reset or publish your content. The original ROOT02 data remains unchanged, and subsequent MOTION01 edits stay in MOTION01. If the earlier review exists elsewhere, do not move raw database files blindly; keep the old folder for a reviewed import. Older incompatible student/Horizon data is not adopted. A missing previous ROOT02 dataset starts the normal synthetic review content. This is local review data, not a production migration or backup.

## What works and what remains

The company/audience pages, CMS published-content and private-preview connections, Knowledge articles, local inquiry submission and consent-based analytics are part of the local review. Quote submission asks the team to prepare a quote; it creates no account, charge or automatic registration.

Client Account/Admin dashboards, customer Toolbox and Workspace tools remain planned. No provider integration, deployment, paid service or finished commercial platform is implied. Phase1/legal/provider/independent security/QA/release gates remain open. Windows owner review is still required. Do not deploy or change Cloudflare resources without exact owner authorization.
